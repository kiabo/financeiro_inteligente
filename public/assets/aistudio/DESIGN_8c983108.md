## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-grade operational environment engineered for C-suite decision-makers and Chief Financial Officers. The aesthetic prioritizes absolute precision, high data density, and quiet structural authority over ornamental visual devices.

### Design Movements & Philosophy
The interface operates under a refined **Corporate Modern Minimalist** paradigm:
- **Utilitarian Clarity**: Uncompromising alignment and clear spatial structure eliminate visual friction, allowing instant cognitive absorption of complex metrics.
- **Architectural Restraint**: The UI relies on crisp dividers, subtle tonal shifts, and rigorous typography rather than decorative illustrations or heavy drop shadows.
- **Institutional Weight**: Anchored by deep petroleum slate and grounded neutral foundations, evoking institutional reliability, auditability, and fiscal governance.

### Emotional Target
The user experience must instill confidence, total governance, and analytical composure under high-stakes financial review.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast, functional readability, and restrained semantic signaling across intensive data grids.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F374A`)**: Deep Petroleum Teal. Applied selectively to primary calls to action, selected navigation states, key metric highlights, and executive summary headers.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`)**: Slate Charcoal. Serves as structural emphasis, active table row highlights, and deep secondary navigation controls.
- **Tertiary / Positive Accent (`#059669`)**: Muted Emerald. Reserved strictly for positive financial delta, growth indicators, healthy EBITDA margins, and solvent ledger entries. Paired with soft tint backgrounds (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Critical & Risk Alerts (`#EF4444`)**: Balanced Crimson. Applied to deficit positions, covenant breaches, and downward variance. Paired with light tint backgrounds (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Warning & Deviation (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: Amber Coral. Flags audit discrepancies, pending reconciliations, or budget warnings.
- **Neutral Foundation**:
  - App Canvas: `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` (ice white/subtle warm slate).
  - Cards & Workspaces: `#FFFFFF` (pure optical white).
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`.
  - Body Text: `#334155`.
  - Primary Readouts & Headers: `#0F172A`.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility across dense data tables, micro-labels, and executive KPIs.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Tabular Figures**: Every numeric representation—financial ledgers, balances, timestamps, and percentages—must employ the OpenType tabular numeric feature (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1, 'cv05' 1`).
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Headers use tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) to consolidate typographic mass and maintain clean vertical alignment against tabular content.
- **Hierarchy Control**: Small-scale labels (`label-sm`, `0.6875rem`) use uppercase transformation with positive letter-spacing (`0.04em`) to establish crisp section separators without taking vertical space.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a compact 4px baseline rhythm optimized for analytical data density.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Tables and executive cockpit views span 12 or 8/4 asymmetrical layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Metric strips switch from 4-across to 2x2 configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins. Dense data tables enable horizontal swipe with pinned frozen identifier columns.

### Density Tiers
- **Table Density**: Standard row height is `36px` to `40px` with vertical cell padding constrained to `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- **Panel Insets**: Analytical cards use `space-lg` (16px) or `space-xl` (24px) internal padding to maintain separation without wasting canvas area.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp surface layering and low-contrast borders instead of dramatic drop shadows.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Base Canvas**: Surface tinted `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Tables, Panels)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered with a 1px solid hairline (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow is ultra-soft: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Date Pickers)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), elevated using `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Executive Drill-downs)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered in `#CBD5E1`, backdrop overlay tinted `#0F172A` at 40% opacity, shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, disciplined border radiuses (`roundedness: 1`). Soft 4px curves provide subtle modern softening while retaining the structured posture of high-reliability data grids.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Table Rows)**: `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Surface Containers (Data Cards, Modal Dialogs, Analytical Charts)**: `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Large Overlays & Cockpit Drawers**: `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Form Controls (Checkboxes, Toggles)**: Strict 2px to 4px corners; circle profiles reserved solely for radio options and status pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F374A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `transparent`. Hover: `#164E63`. Pressed: `#0B2735`. Height `36px` (`32px` in compact mode).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`. Hover: background `#FEF2F2`.

### Data Cards & Metric Panels
- Structure: 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`), background `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`.
- Card Header: Compact title (`title-md`), uppercase metadata tag (`label-sm`), and action icon button aligned on the right.
- KPI Values: Expressed in `tabular-metric-lg`, immediately followed by a delta badge (`+4.2% vs budget`).

### Financial Tables
- Header Row: Background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, label styling `label-md` with color `#64748B`.
- Content Rows: Alternating row hover state `#F1F5F9`, border bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- Numeric Columns: Text aligned right, font family `Inter` with enabled tabular figures.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Positive / Margin Safe**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **Alert / Overrun**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Neutral / Pending**: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Form Inputs & Date Range Selectors
- Heights: Consistent `36px` vertical height.
- Resting State: Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, radius `4px`.
